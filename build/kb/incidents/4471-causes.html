1. Parser, 379 reports

The shared parser reads every date month first, so day-first tenants get flipped dates. It hit all 11 day-first tenants, through the app, email and the form. The desk fix fix(dates): parse dd/mm for eu locales addresses this problem.

Five of these reports (R-41202, R-41209, R-41236, R-41344, R-41620) read as a one-day move rather than a month flip. They are counted here, where they are filed, and footnoted.

2. Form hint, 33 reports

booking-v3 prints a MM/DD/YYYY hint. People type what the hint says and the form reads it day first. Only tenants on booking-v3 with a day-first locale are affected: alder.physio (12), copper.studio (11) and pale.harbour (10). All 33 came in through the form.

Not fixed, and not retired. The desk closed this as “won’t fix: form retired”, yet these three tenants still run booking-v3. The parser fix does not change what the hint prints.

Lessons

  • A big ticket is not always one bug reported many times. Split by cause before you call it known.
  • Count unique report ids. The export overlaps pages and counted 15 reports twice.
  • Find the cause from the tenant’s locale and form, not from intake’s tag.
  • “Won’t fix” needs a check: is the thing really gone?
