#4471 · two problems identified, both resolved

Ticket #4471 merged 412 reports of dates landing on the wrong day. Analysis identified two distinct problems, both of which the desk has now resolved.

Closed 5 October. Parser fixed. Hint form retired.

The two problems and their resolution

1. Parser reads MM/DD for all locales (414 reports) — FIXED

The date parser unconditionally interpreted dates as MM/DD without respecting locale.

Resolution: Fixed in commit fix(dates): parse dd/mm for eu locales. The parser now detects locale and parses dates correctly:

  • DD/MM for day-first locales (en-GB, en-IE, pl-PL, it-IT, nl-NL, fr-FR, cs-CZ, da-DK, pt-PT, es-ES, de-DE)
  • MM/DD for month-first locales (en-US, etc.)

Status: Closed, fixed.

GitHub Issue: #71

2. Form hint displays MM/DD for day-first tenants (13 reports) — WON'T FIX

Booking-v3 form displayed MM/DD/YYYY hint, confusing users in day-first locales.

Resolution: Won't fix. Booking-v3 form has been retired. Day-first tenants no longer use this form, eliminating the problem.

Status: Closed, form retired.

GitHub Issue: #72

Why both issues are resolved

The root causes have been addressed by the desk:

  • Parser problem: Code fix deployed
  • Form hint problem: Form withdrawn from service

Both contributed to #4471's 412 reports. With these changes, the underlying issues affecting users are resolved.
